## Files

- `dist/content.js` and `dist/content-bonding.js` / `dist/content-covalent.js`: original teaching content and questions.
- `dist/registry.js`: subjects’ science courses/topics, topic membership and run-context migration.
- `dist/widgets.js` / `dist/widgets-covalent.js`: interactive investigations; `dist/covalent-builder.js` validates outer-shell diagrams.
- `dist/visual.js`: local Three.js rendering and accessible schematic/2D views.
- `dist/progress.js`: progress, scoring, pathway filtering and review scheduling.
- `dist/app.js`: navigation, activity flows, assessments and persistence.
- `tests/progress.test.js`: curriculum validation and learning-state regression tests.

Run `node --test tests/*.test.js`. Preview with `python3 -m http.server 4173 --directory dist`.

Progress is local to the browser. The previous version’s course/tier preferences and legacy activity evidence are preserved when migrating, but changed lessons do not inherit an unearned mastery score. First assessment answers are stored separately from practice retries. Numeric questions reject blanks/non-finite values and use explicit tolerances. Assessment and review runs are kept separately by topic, pathway and tier; existing v2 atomic progress and unfinished runs migrate without renaming existing IDs. New concepts are reviewed after a day; successful recall expands the interval to 3, 9 and 21 days. Misses are immediately eligible for review. Reviews are in-app; no notification automation is created.

The topic uses illustrative models, not precise molecular dynamics. Apparatus and alpha-scattering diagrams are schematic. Shell filling is limited to the first 20 elements. No simulation fulfils the school practical requirement. The workspace curriculum directory contains all Chemistry objective/coverage records and the completion-run QA record. Technical checks are not specialist content review or evidence of physical practical competence.
