## Model and motion decision (required)

- Learning purpose: structure / process / evidence comparison. Why this representation?
- Would a short repeatable process film explain direction, sequence, transfer or transformation better than a still? Camera rotation does not count as process motion.
- If a film: define stages, moving entities, conserved quantities, direction, beginning/end/reset, and what the timing does and does not mean.
- Plan play/pause, replay, scrub, stage stepping, optional loop/speed and readable transcript; open inline and autoplay with looping enabled (start paused when reduced motion is requested). Preserve accessible still diagrams and user-led prediction.
- State whether this is a worked example or reflects the actual experiment controls. Never imply that replay resets nature or changes a saved experiment.
- Verify course/tier scope, reduced-motion still use, phone/keyboard, pause/close/background, and that watching cannot earn completion or reveal independent answers.

Mixed lessons: explicitly mark structure/data explanation cards `process: false` and supply the actual diagram/data scene. They open on that evidence; process cards and process investigations use inline autoplay/loop. Verify switching between these card types. Tier-specific activity requirements must be checked at completion, not inferred from a shared widgetDone flag.
