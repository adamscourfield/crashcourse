## Exact observation bank

Flames: lithium **crimson**, sodium **yellow**, potassium **lilac**, calcium **orange-red**, copper **green**. Preserve these scientific colours despite family UI palette; accompany every colour with its text label.

NaOH precipitates: Al3+, Ca2+, Mg2+ **white**; only aluminium hydroxide dissolves in excess NaOH. Cu2+ **blue**, Fe2+ **green**, Fe3+ **brown**. Calcium versus magnesium cannot be resolved from white precipitate alone: combine another valid test/data. Do not silently add ammonia solubility or ammonium testing as required material.

Acidified silver nitrate: chloride **white AgCl**, bromide **cream AgBr**, iodide **yellow AgI**. Acidified barium chloride: sulfate **white BaSO4**. State reagent identity with the observation; “white precipitate” alone is ambiguous.

Equation authoring checks (both-tier molecular examples): CuSO4 + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)2 + Na2SO4; FeCl2 + 2NaOH → Fe(OH)2 + 2NaCl; FeCl3 + 3NaOH → Fe(OH)3 + 3NaCl; AlCl3 + 3NaOH → Al(OH)3 + 3NaCl; MgCl2 + 2NaOH → Mg(OH)2 + 2NaCl; CaCl2 + 2NaOH → Ca(OH)2 + 2NaCl. Add states when appropriate. Optional Higher ionic representations follow M(n+) + nOH− → M(OH)n(s), with proper charges and atom conservation; they must not gate Foundation completion.

## Practical and model design constraints

Chromatography setup: small concentrated spots on a pencil baseline above solvent; solvent wets paper base; keep paper from vessel sides; avoid disturbing apparatus. Mark solvent front promptly before it disappears, dry paper and measure consistently from baseline. Ink baseline may dissolve; submerged samples wash into solvent; broad spots worsen centre measurement. Compare references using the same solvent/conditions. Large Rf means greater travel in this system, not greater mass or universal solubility. Rf=30/60=0.50 is a suitable original worked example; independent checks need fresh numbers. Include estimate, ratio, measured diagram distances, significant figures and interpretation; Rf calculations are **both tiers**.

Ion-test planning: use fresh portions so one reagent cannot contaminate the next test, clean flame wire between samples and compare known references before unknowns. Acid choice matters: HCl introduces chloride and invalidates halide testing; acidification removes carbonate interference before precipitation tests. The last explanations are methodological reasoning attached to the specified tests, not additional named test requirements. Handbook's unknown is a single ionic compound, not a universal unrestricted forensic mixture. Simulated qualitative tests can model discrimination and uncertainty but cannot certify physical technique or replace school RP6/7 (Trilogy RP12). School supervision/risk assessment applies; avoid presenting hazardous gas generation as home activities. Chlorine's test is observation-based and does not require directing students to smell it.

Spectroscopy: labelled reference wavelengths/patterns; match positions, not decorative RGB alone. Calibration must explicitly provide intensity/concentration relationship under comparable conditions; line identity establishes ions while quantitative signal calibration establishes concentration. Do not claim every brighter line universally means higher concentration across different elements. “Sensitive” means detects small amounts; “rapid” means fast; accuracy means close to accepted value. No need to teach atomic transitions/absorption spectra or mass spectroscopy beyond supported optional context.

## Proposed coherent lesson set

1. **Purity and formulations** — shared both-tier; classify substances/products and diagnose melting data; component-proportion design comparison. A01–04.
2. **Chromatography and Rf** — shared both-tier; model distribution, move measurement rulers, compute Rf and compare reference/unknown lanes; challenge overlapping spots with second solvent. A05–08.
3. **Chromatography practical preparation** — shared both-tier; setup diagnosis, practical sequence, original result table/measurements, evaluation. A09 with A06–08 retrieval.
4. **Identifying gases** — shared both-tier; predict test then reveal labelled observation and choose justified inference, covering all four gases. A10–13.
5. **Flame tests and metal hydroxides** — separate both-tier; branching unknown cation evidence, excess-NaOH distinction, balanced molecular equation construction. A14–16.
6. **Anion tests and unknown salts** — separate both-tier; reagent selection/fresh aliquots, all three halides, carbonate and sulfate, combined cation/anion unknown RP7 preparation. A17–20 with A14–16 retrieval.
7. **Instrumental analysis and flame emission** — separate both-tier; interpret line-reference overlays and concentration calibration data, compare instrumental advantages. A21–23.

These are proposed stable scope boundaries; implementation should create named lesson/question/concept IDs and map every A objective to Discover, meaningful investigation and fresh independent evidence. Existing atomic chromatography should remain prerequisite/retrieval rather than duplicate isolated progress.
