## Learning purpose
Identify pathogen groups; distinguish routes, controls and damage; explain the specified disease examples and human defences. Prerequisite cell types, then pathogens. Misconceptions: mosquito=pathogen; HIV=AIDS; antibiotics=antibodies; viral replication=bacterial division; toxin neutralisation=engulfment. Each is diagnosed in activities/practice/checks with explanatory correction.

## Discover
Three or four short cards per lesson. Examples are measles/HIV/TMV; Salmonella/gonorrhoea; rose black spot/malaria. Follow damage→effect and route→intervention reasoning. Original 3D cutaways paired with labelled SVG; symbolic colour, scale and discrete stages, not diagnosis, measured rates or clinical advice. Viruses replicate inside living cells. Stomach acid kills many, not all, pathogens.

## Investigate
Each lesson has three or four challenges. Select a model state or intervention, predict its consequence, reveal the change, then record correct reasoning across both comparisons. State changes alter actual geometry/route visibility. Pathogen types require all four groups; viral case comparisons connect replication, immune-cell effects and leaf damage; bacterial cases compare hygiene, barriers and susceptibility; malaria distinguishes bite-blocking from breeding control; defences compare barriers, ciliary clearance, engulfment, specific binding and antitoxin action. Wrong answers explain mechanisms and remain retryable. Choices and reveal state persist; completion requires all cases. Native selects/buttons support keyboard. Still models by default, manually stepped; 2D/text alternative and non-colour labels.
