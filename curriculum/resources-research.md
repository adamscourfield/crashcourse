Proposed lesson boundaries: (1) changing atmosphere and evidence, (2) photosynthesis and carbon stores, (3) greenhouse effect, (4) climate evidence and consequences, (5) footprints and reduction, (6) combustion pollution. Require independent questions across every table row; headings alone are insufficient.

Authoring facts: photosynthesis `6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂`; distinguish greenhouse warming from ozone depletion and global dimming. Teach approximate 80:20 as specification approximation, not an exact air composition. Carbon monoxide/soot result from incomplete combustion; sulfur impurities produce SO₂; atmospheric nitrogen and oxygen react at high engine temperatures. CO₂ examples: fossil-fuel combustion, deforestation; methane: livestock and landfill decomposition. Four effects can be sea-level rise, rainfall-pattern changes, extreme-weather changes and habitat/species-distribution changes. Avoid asserting that uncertainty cancels the evidence or that every individual weather event proves climate change.

Skills: WS1.1–1.6 (as relevant), WS3.5/3.6/4.1, MS1c. Photosynthetic oxygen demonstration is an opportunity, not a required practical in this topic.

## Separate Chemistry Haber/NPK

| Ref | Tier | Required scope |
|---|---|---|
|4.10.4.1|Both|Ammonia manufacture, nitrogen/hydrogen sources, iron catalyst, about450°C/about200atm; reversible reaction; cooling liquefies ammonia and unreacted gases recycled.|
|4.10.4.1 HT statements|Higher|Read rate/condition graphs; dynamic-equilibrium application; rate/yield trade-off and commercial choices considering materials/energy costs.|
|4.10.4.2|Both|NPK salt formulations/element percentages; integrated industrial processes; ammonia → ammonium salts/nitric acid; mined KCl/K₂SO₄/phosphate rock; rock acid treatment; name products for three acids; compare industrial/laboratory preparations. AT4 opportunity: ammonium salt preparation.|

Proposed lessons: Haber production (both tiers); Haber compromise (Higher); NPK processes and salt preparation (both tiers). `N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃`; forward exothermic. Air supplies nitrogen; methane/natural gas supplies hydrogen. Higher: cooling favours equilibrium yield but slows production; pressure favours two gas molecules over four but increases costs; catalyst improves rate without changing equilibrium position. Ammonia withdrawal/recycling increases overall conversion without claiming a single-pass100% yield.

Official assessment corroboration for fertiliser products: [2023 Paper2H mark scheme,10.3–10.6](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/sample-papers-and-mark-schemes/2023/june/AQA-84622H-MS-JUN23.PDF), [2021 Paper2H mark scheme,05.2](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/sample-papers-and-mark-schemes/2021/november/AQA-84622H-MS-NOV21.PDF). Rock + nitric acid gives calcium nitrate (and phosphoric acid); sulfuric-acid treatment gives single superphosphate (calcium sulfate with phosphate); phosphoric-acid treatment gives triple superphosphate (soluble calcium phosphate form). Avoid implying insoluble original rock and soluble fertiliser phosphate are structurally identical; optional precision: calcium dihydrogen phosphate. NPK denotes elements in compounds, not elemental N/P/K mixed together. Ammonia plus nitric acid gives ammonium nitrate; ammonia plus sulfuric acid gives ammonium sulfate. Laboratory soluble-acid/alkali preparation can use titration to identify neutralising volumes, repeat without indicator, concentrate/cool/crystallise; industrial integrated scale/continuous control is a distinct comparison.

## Interaction and assessment design recommendations

Use a linked cause/evidence timeline rather than an invented exact early-atmosphere percentage chart. Greenhouse interaction should track incoming shortwave and outgoing longwave energy with an explicit simplified-model label. Pollutant work should require both cause and consequence. Water should require choosing treatment for a stated impurity, not merely ordering arbitrary buttons. LCA decisions should expose system boundaries and repeated-use assumptions. Materials should require a justified choice against a data table. Haber should distinguish rate and equilibrium displays; Foundation must never receive Higher condition-shift assessment. Supply fresh numeric datasets for independent assessment and retain the existing stable-ID progress model.
