## Purpose, prerequisites and misconceptions
Explain reactants/products and endothermic light transfer to chloroplasts; recognise CO₂/H₂O/O₂/C₆H₁₂O₆; explain single-factor rate effects and chlorophyll amount; Higher interpret interacting factors, inverse-square and cost/profit; plan/measure/calculate/plot pondweed rate evidence; connect glucose to respiration, starch, fats/oils, cellulose and amino-acid/protein synthesis with nitrate. Prerequisite shared Organisation plant-tissues and enzyme-action/conditions; later lessons use photosynthesis. Preserve prior IDs and topic-scoped progress.

Misconceptions: light is a material reactant/creates atoms; oxygen is the sugar; plants get all food mass from soil; hotter is always faster; bubble number always equals volume; distance is light intensity itself; inverse-square always predicts the biological rate; highest rate is highest profit; glucose alone supplies nitrogen; plants only respire at night. Diagnose via predictions, graph/data tasks and fresh checks, with explanatory correction.

## Discover
Three/four concise cards per lesson. Original3D chloroplast cutaway with grana, material/energy routes and atom inventories; controlled condition models; apparatus/lamp spacing; glucose allocation. Analytical work uses labelled plots/tables rather than forced3D. Model counts, organelle sizes and stage spacing symbolic. Original rate curves/data not biological calibration. No molecular mechanism or thylakoid chemistry required. Temperature optimum conditional; simulated observations do not establish school practical competence.

## Investigate
Reaction: construct word/formula relations, inspect a conserved inventory and compare light/dark energy transfer. Factors: change light, CO₂, temperature and chlorophyll independently with predictions and numeric readouts/one-factor curves; no multi-factor inference required on Foundation. Higher: compare two/three-factor curve conditions, calculate inverse-square comparisons and evaluate greenhouse net returns from supplied costs/revenue. Practical: choose controls/safe setup, move lamp and collect repeated oxygen-volume readings after acclimatisation, calculate mean/rate and build a constrained graph with units/scale; evaluate bubble-size/count and oxygen/net-rate limitations. Glucose: route products to uses/storage/wall/protein and test nitrate dependency while preserving the light/respiration distinction.

Every task needs correct reasoning and comparison evidence; wrong feedback is retryable. Models start still and advance by explicit buttons; native controls support keyboard. Labelled SVG/text and raw tables accompany3D/colour. Save model/experiment/numeric/plot drafts; completion cannot be earned solely by watching. Balance conservation is a cross-topic scaffold beyond symbol recognition, not mislabelled an explicit separate AQA requirement.
