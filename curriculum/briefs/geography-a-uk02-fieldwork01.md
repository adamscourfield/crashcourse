## Discover and misconception diagnostics

Prior prompts connect management to pressure, projections to assumptions, and a field question to observable evidence. Sequence: define, trace worked mechanism/data, then judge limits. Park protection does not ban all development; flood measures do not remove all risk; an interval is not a guaranteed future; a hypothesis need not be confirmed; more biased observations do not remove bias; correlation is not causation.
