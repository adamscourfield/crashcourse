## Learning purpose and misconceptions
Develop a directional hypothesis with collision reasoning; isolate concentration with controlled volume, temperature and solid surface. Read gas volume over time using a gas-tight delivery system/gas syringe, distinguish initial rate from final amount with fixed limiting metal and excess acid. Explain turbidity from sulfur formation in thiosulfate/acid, timed disappearing-cross endpoint; fixed depth/lighting/observer, consistent mixing/timing and repeats. Reciprocal endpoint time is a relative rate index only for a comparable fixed endpoint, not a gas-volume rate. Diagnose leaks, late sealing, subjectivity and anomalies. Counter changing total volume, using final yield as speed, interpreting longer endpoint time as faster, gas escaping as lower chemistry yield, or1/t as mol/s.
