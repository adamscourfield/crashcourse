## Learning purpose and misconceptions
Plan a fair investigation of acid concentration versus solution temperature rise; distinguish independent/dependent/control variables; use an insulated cup/lid, mixing, initial reading and maximum/minimum; calculate temperature differences, interpret concentration trend and evaluate heat loss/anomalous repeats. Counter maximum temperature versus change, varying total volume or starting temperature, automatic outlier deletion and better insulation proving exact energy.
Higher: bond breaking takes energy; bond formation releases it. Count all bonds using balanced coefficients, total supplied bond energies, calculate broken minus formed, interpret sign and relative magnitudes. Counter reversing subtraction, omitting coefficients, counting molecules as bonds and confusing activation energy with overall change.
