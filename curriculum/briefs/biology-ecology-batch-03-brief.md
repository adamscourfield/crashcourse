## Learning purpose / misconceptions / coverage
Trace materials between biotic/abiotic stores; carbon photosynthesis/feeding/death/respiration/decomposition and mineral-ion return, water evaporation/condensation/precipitation/runoff/groundwater/transpiration, importance of fresh water and reused building blocks. Matter cycles; energy transfers do not imply energy recycles in the same way. Nitrogen cycle excluded. Explain temperature/moisture/oxygen effects on decay, optimum context/denaturation, compost as fertiliser and anaerobic methane/biogas fuel. Calculate original mass-loss rates and compare experimental conditions. Plan/interpret RP10 temperature versus pH/time, controlled quantities, equilibration, endpoint/repeats, graph/rate and limitations. Lipase model fatty-acid pH fall is distinct from natural lactic-acid decay. Evaluate distribution from supplied temperature/water/gas evidence; seasonal/geographic/human contexts and uncertainty. No precise universal decay optimum, simulated cure/safety claim or real ecological forecast.

Prerequisites: communities/abiotic, carbon before decay, prior enzyme conditions for milk model. Misconceptions: plants only photosynthesise; decomposition destroys matter; minerals are energy; condensation makes water vanish; more heat always speeds decay; oxygen required for every decay pathway; lipase is a bacterium; pH differences directly equal acid quantities; a threshold map proves observed abundance/extinction.
