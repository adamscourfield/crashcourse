## Learning purpose and prerequisites
After circulation, heat transfer, past climate and proxy lessons, students explain natural versus enhanced infrared energy transfer, link industry/transport/energy/farming to CO₂ and CH₄, explain variable crop yields and sea-level/glacier effects, read measured temperature line/bar charts and evaluate spatial/temporal coverage, and explain UK regional climate through location, latitude, altitude, relief, maritime influence and prevailing winds. No assumption that an excluded landscape was completed.

Misconceptions: all radiation blocked → diagnose greenhouse check3; ozone hole causes greenhouse warming → check4; all farming emits one gas → sources check3; no exhaust means no lifecycle emissions → practice3; every crop benefits/fails → impacts check1; floating sea ice explains all sea-level rise → sea views/practice2; retreat means uphill ice flow → impacts check3; Central England equals allUK/global → history task1/practice3; one cold year overturns trend → history task4/check3;1000-year thermometer record → history check4; prevailing wind means permanent direction → controls practice3.
