## Twenty learning purposes, prerequisites and misconceptions

1.Historymethods: contrast recurrent taught account and longer recovered portraits, sound, typography and light/vision; Creole is crafted, not erroneous. Prereq14-1/fullassignedpoem. Compare Emigree language/identity without equating experiences.
2.Kamikazemeaning: narrated daughter’s family account and imagined father’s view; departure, nature/memory, return, ostracism. Prereqfullpoem. Return is explicit, pilot’s precise motive inferred; no clinical/sweeping claim about all Japanese people.
3.Kamikazemethods: seven sestets, enjambment, narration→daughter/directfamilyvoice, expansive nature/detail versus learned silence; compare BayonetCharge duties/survival without equating outcome. Prereq15-1.
4.Powerauthority: Duchess/Ozymandias, active self-presentation versus surviving broken representation; compare voices/frames, not simply 'both powerful'.
5.Nature: Prelude/Storm, remembered individual awe versus communal conversational threat; do not identify speaker and poet or force identical response.

8.Identity: Emigree/History, persistent remembered language versus active historical recovery; dialect not lack of skill; unnamed city remains unnamed.
