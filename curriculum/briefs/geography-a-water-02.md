## Discover and misconceptions
Three concise cards per lesson, actual cardViews bound to static evidence or a relevant process. Rainfall is not safe delivered supply; leaking mains differ from combined-sewer overflow; no safely-managed service does not mean everyone drinks surface water; withdrawal differs from consumption; development is not a universal sector-share rule; high stress is not a percentage of people without taps; reverse osmosis does not boil water; desalination does not destroy salt. Diagnostic options/feedback target these confusions. Original hypothetical values and schematic limitations visible. Worked examples:100−20=80delivered;100feed−40product=60brine;3saltunits/60volume is higher concentration than3/100, idealised salt-free product; savings100→70=30%. Independent checks use fresh120−25,120feed−45product and60→70coverage examples.
