## Objectives and misconceptions
Distinguish fusion of gametes and genetic mixing from one-parent mitotic cloning; recognise sperm/egg and pollen-carried male gametes. Explain one DNA replication and two divisions, four single-set genetically different products, fertilisation restoring the set and mitotic embryo growth/differentiation. Distinguish DNA-copy number from chromosome number. Compare reproductive strategy benefits/costs with environmental change and required malaria/fungi/strawberry/daffodil examples. Define DNA double helix/polymer, chromosomes, gene coding for amino-acid sequence/protein, genome and three human genome applications. Pollen carries male gametes; it is not the whole male gamete itself. Outcomes in the gamete model are generalised: four products shown, not four functional eggs from every animal meiosis.
