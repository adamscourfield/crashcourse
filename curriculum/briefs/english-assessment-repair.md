## Learning purpose and prerequisites

Student can retrieve accurately, explain a context-specific method and defend a reading using fresh evidence. Prior lessons teach observation/inference, local context and evidence selection. Diagnostic samples these before harder transfers; a wrong inference leads to rereading and evidence tracing, not a generic restart. Misconceptions: a plausible motive is a fact; any quotation proves a claim; a feature always has one effect; critical means negative; structure is just a list of devices. Use distinct plausible errors for each actual passage.
