## Learning purposes and prerequisites
Shared landscape weather/climate concepts useful; no requirement to complete an excluded landscape. Explain named Hadley/Ferrel/Polar cells, pressure/rising/sinking/wind belts with approximately0/30/60/90latitude. Explain unequal heating and poleward net heat transfer via atmosphere and ocean. Compare Quaternary glacials/interglacials across time scales. Distinguish orbital distribution, solar intensity and volcanic aerosol mechanisms. Interpret ice-core gases versus temperature proxies, pollen vegetation proxies, tree-ring growth limits and historical-source bias; corroborate records. Avoid permanent six-cell weather forecast, direct thermometers in every proxy, orbit-only recent warming, uniform cycle timing and inference of a full trend from one event.
