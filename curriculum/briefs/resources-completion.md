## Learning purpose
Each lesson's `learn` cards define observable explanations to build, `lab` fields require a decision/calculation and each independent check uses fresh prompts. Prerequisites are general particle,equation and data-reading ideas; advanced Haber assumes reversible reactions/equilibrium. Deliberately avoid hard dependency IDs until primary confirms integration ordering.

Misconceptions are diagnosed in options and corrected by each question's `why`: confusion between evidence and certainty, omission of process stages, incorrect denominators, composition versus properties and rate versus equilibrium. Full card/objective text is retained in the requirements CSV for coverage audit; lesson availability does not itself prove independent mastery of every written skill.
