## Learning purpose and exact scope
| ID | Ref | Observable objective and misconception diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| slopes |1.7a weathering/mass movement|Explain mechanical, chemical, biological breakdown in place; compare slide/rotational slump moving material downhill under gravity; connect saturation and river toe erosion to stability. Not every wet slope fails.|
| sediment |1.7a erosion/transport/deposition|Identify hydraulic action, abrasion, attrition and solution; compare rolling/bouncing/suspended/dissolved load and reduced transport capacity; distinguish erosion of banks/bed from particle collisions.|
| downstream |1.7b|Compare upper/middle/lower valley profile, channel width/depth and gradient in broad tendency; explain downward vs lateral erosion, tributary accumulation and alluvium; distinguish valley cross-section from longitudinal profile. Trends are not universal rules.|
| tees-flow |1.7b|Locate Tees source Cross Fell/North Pennines and North Sea mouth; explain discharge vs velocity, changing sediment size/roundness and channel efficiency; work Q=A×v in explicitly hypothetical reaches. Never present invented width/depth/flow as Tees measurements. Human modifications complicate simple textbook trends.|
| weather |1.7c|Explain storms/saturation/lag and drought/evapotranspiration, seasonality vs single events; compare heavy rain over absorbent vs saturated catchments. Read a genuine EA gauge excerpt with units/date/type and reject a causal or discharge inference from stage extrema. Authentic paired UK rainfall/climate series and full skill10 evidence remain open.|

## Discover / model and motion decisions
Three causal cards each; numerical worked examples in flow lesson. Models compare mechanisms with direction/time: breakdown/sliding/slumping; rolling/bouncing/suspended sediment; static channel/valley cross-section comparisons; discharge particle flux; catchment runoff and drought. Default loop, pause, manual beginning/middle/end and Loop-off supported. Reduced-motion starts paused. Every view has labels and a textual caption; symbolic dimensions/time scales declared. The weather record view stays static and automatically pauses: two separately dated observations are not interpolated into an invented time series. Landform/photo/case expansion later, no decorative generated place photographs needed here.

## Investigate
Four challenges per lesson: predict a contrasting outcome before selecting its view; compare evidence, interpret cause or units, reject an overclaim. Require all view selections AND correct challenges. Wrong answers correctable; incomplete continuation blocked. Discharge controls compare fixed-area/fixed-speed cases, enabling a controlled comparison rather than implying all downstream variables cause every result. Manual stages reversible; no calibrated forecast or real field measurement claim.
