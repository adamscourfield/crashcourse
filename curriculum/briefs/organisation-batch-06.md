## Discover / learning purpose

Original short sequences connect cellular structure to whole-plant transport. No detailed phloem loading or pressure-flow mechanism is required. Root/shoot growth meristems included; leaf vocabulary stays within specified tissues and guard cells. Worked examples distinguish bubble distance/time from volume/time; projected field area and sampled stomatal density use matching units. Potometer uptake approximates transpiration but is not identical to water loss. Observations are original simulations, not measurements or physical AT competence.
