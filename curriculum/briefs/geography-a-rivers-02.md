High Force photograph links generalised geology to a genuine named feature without claiming a measured reconstruction. Hydrograph worked example: same invented rainfall peak hour1; urban discharge peak36m³/s at hour2, rural20m³/s at hour4. Urban lag1hour, rural3hours; discharge-peak timing difference2hours. Different quantities are not interchangeable. Graph uses labelled rainfall bars, discharge axes/units, solid versus dashed curves and textual data alternative.

Waterfall: moving water, growing undercut, falling block; retreat view shifts lip upstream, former lip dashed, gorge downstream. Generic rock geometry/time compressed, not an actual High Force section. Spurs remain a static plan. Cliff/point-bar section juxtaposes outer erosion and inner deposition.

Deposits: overbank rise/fall and settling particles, coarse near bank; repeated event accretion is compressed and exaggerated. Meander: compare banks, narrowing neck, high-flow cut-through, sealed entrances/isolated loop. Actual shortcut connects incoming/outgoing limbs; loop replays a possible sequence, not a probability or literal repeated event. Full three-dimensional secondary flow simplified.

Flooding: rain/runoff/infiltration on permeable/urban/farm/industrial surfaces. Same-storm hydrograph stays static: animation must not imply observed measurements. Management: finite reservoir storage/controlled release, channelised conveyance, washland fill/release; zoning static exposure comparison. No engineering model or protection percentages implied.
