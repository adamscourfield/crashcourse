## Learning purpose and mapping
Students locate brain regions and relate them to function; evaluate brain investigation/treatment evidence (Higher); identify eye structures and functions; distinguish pupil control from accommodation; link muscle/ligament/lens changes; interpret defective and corrected ray diagrams; compare correction technologies. See biology-senses-requirements.csv for individual assessable statements and evidence. Prerequisite: nervous-system lesson.

## Discover
Three concise cards per lesson. Brain3D and labelled side view; eye3D cutaway and labelled2D; pupil, lens/ray and correction films. Figures are schematic and enlarged, not clinical images, calibrated optics or anatomy measurements. Structure views retain manual camera controls. Evidence cards distinguish observation, inference and uncertainty.

## Investigate
Eleven challenges, each requiring all supplied scenarios: predict, reveal evidence, correct reasoning and record each case. Brain regional identification/function, three investigative methods and risk evaluation, eye structures and pupil response, near/far muscle–ligament–lens chain, focusing misconception diagnosis, myopia/hyperopia correction and technologies. Wrong selections cannot earn completion. Native labelled selects/buttons support keyboard; selected scenario, prediction, reveal, recorded cases and challenge index persist in sensesDrafts. Later recall uses the existing concept scheduler.

## Practice and independent check
20 feedback-led practice items and20 fresh independent checks. Stable question/concept IDs in source. Answer keys and explanations are explicit. Independent brain A/B/C and ray stimuli do not label the diagnosis or correct choice; process player is excluded. First answers lock,80% threshold retained, retakes recorded separately. Recognition/selected reasoning evidence does not establish independent extended writing or freehand ray drawing.

## Motion decision
Brain structure uses inspectable3D and labelled2D; brain-method evidence stays a controlled comparison. Eye light response, accommodation and correcting focus use three short auto-looping worked films. Pupil aperture/lens width change and tracer direction are illustrative; correctors show two separate worked cases. Controls preserve pause, replay, stage, scrub, speed, loop and transcript. Reduced motion starts paused. Films do not award completion or overwrite an investigation; input switches back to chosen evidence. Real light speed, muscle dynamics and prescription calculations are not simulated.
