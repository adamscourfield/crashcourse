## Objectives and misconceptions

- Duchess04-2: analyse rhyming pentameter couplets, enjambment/caesura, Duke’s performance and reader inference; regular rhyme does not guarantee psychological control; compare possession with Ozymandias. Prereq04-1.
- Charge05-1: distinguish order/error, action, survival/loss and commemoration; honouring courage does not endorse every order or mean all600died. CrimeanWar/Balaclava1854 context supports reading; not an eyewitness transcript.
- Charge05-2: link emphatic recurring rhythm, repetition, changing refrain and finalimperatives with collective momentum and public memory; not mechanically identical metre throughout. Compare Exposure’s stalled collective waiting. Prereqfullpoem,05-1; readExposure alongside comparison.
- Exposure06-1: trace collective cold/waiting, memories ofhome, faith/duty tension and death; refrain does not mean no harm. Differentiate poeticvoice from complete diaryrecord.
- Exposure06-2: analyse five-line stanza pattern, shortfinal lines, recurringrefrain and pararhyme; refrain wording not identical in every stanza. Compare bodily effects of weather with Prelude or heroiccommemoration with Charge. Prereq06-1.
- Storm07-1: trace shared confidence, lackofshelter, imaginedcompanionship and fear; unseen wind is physicallyreal, not harmless because invisible. Contextual political reading possible, title resemblance not proof of single allegory.
- Storm07-2: analyse singleverseparagraph/blankverse, conversationaladdress, animalcomparison and militarymetaphors; figurative attack is not evidence of a literal army in scene. Compare uncertain human mastery with Prelude. Prereq07-1.
- Bayonet08-1: trace thirdperson individual running, bodilyshock, hesitation, hare and survival; not necessarily awakeningfromsleep or a namedbattle, poet not automatically soldier.
- Bayonet08-2: analyse inmediasres, threeunevenstanzas/linebreaks, simile and shifting priorities; device effects require local evidence; compare collectiveCharge with isolated focalisation. Prereq08-1.
- Remains09-1: track conversational militaryaccount, disputedthreat, collectiveaction and later firstpersonmemory; threat unresolved, homeleave not instantrecovery, not poet’s literalconfession or automatic clinicaldiagnosis.
