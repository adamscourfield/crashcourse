## Investigate and model/motion decision
Four labelled views and four diagnostic challenges each; predict, compare and explain before continuation. Economic toy shows totalGDP and population increasing proportionately while per-capita value stays fixed. Factor chains move tokens through mechanisms (food/water→health→school/work;technology/connectivity;inclusion;economicopportunity). Income-distribution toy transfers a fixed100units among5people, preserving total/mean; not actual GDP allocation. Physical trade route, historical legacy and cumulative investment loops show direction/sequence, not measured rates or forecasts. Static political/capability checklists, actualGDP/HDI/CPI comparisons, world choropleth and UKregional chart remain still. Motion only where it explains change; inline autoplay+Loop default, shared reduced-motion paused start, Play/Pause and stage controls. Accessible SVG labels/captions, non-colour values/tables, keyboard controls; watching alone cannot earn completion. No photoreal image needed for these quantitative/conceptual goals.
