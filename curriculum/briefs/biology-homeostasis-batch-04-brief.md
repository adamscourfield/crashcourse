## Misconceptions and assessment
Water moves by osmosis, not solute through every membrane. Kidneys regulate urine losses; ADH is not produced in kidney. Urea is made in liver, removed by kidney; it is distinct from urine. Filtration is not selective recovery; useful glucose can enter filtrate and be reabsorbed. Dialysis retains cells/proteins and maintains useful-solute gradients; blood does not mix directly with dialysate. A transplant is a treatment with ongoing monitoring, not a guaranteed permanent cure. Keys, wrong feedback, units and stable concept/question IDs live in source.
