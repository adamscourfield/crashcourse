## Objectives, prerequisites and diagnostic misconceptions

- Remains09-2: analyse conversational voice, uncertain threat, recurring memory, seven quatrains plus final couplet, collective/personal shift; compare War Photographer with attention to different roles. Prereq09-1 and full poem. Short final stanza is not rhyming proof of resolution; poet is not automatically speaker.
- Poppies10-1: distinguish parent voice, remembered leave-taking and later memorial visit; trace private/public remembrance without asserting the child's death, military service or a named war is explicitly established. Prereq full poem and recall of absence in Remains.
- Poppies10-2: analyse textile/bird/military imagery, four uneven stanzas, temporal movement and enjambment; compare personal absence with Remains, preserving parent/participant distinction. Prereq10-1. A metaphor is not documentary proof of a wound or a death.
- War Photographer11-1: distinguish third-person narrator, focal photographer, editor and readers; trace developing images, recollection and selection; distinguish taking/developing photographs. Prereq full poem. Narrator's judgment of audience is not an empirically proven claim about every reader.
- War Photographer11-2: analyse four sestets, ABBCDD end-rhyme arrangement, order versus distress, religious imagery and contrasts of place; compare Remains on return/home and response, without equating photographer and shooter. Prereq11-1/09-1.
- Tissue12-1: trace thought rather than plot from paper records/maps/receipts through imagined architecture to skin; question fixed systems through fragile human life. Prereq full poem. Not a literal instruction to build houses of paper or destroy books; multiple supported readings allowed.
- Tissue12-2: analyse free verse, nine quatrains/final single line, enjambment across stanza boundaries, imagery of light and direct address; compare Ozymandias on permanence through different structures. Prereq12-1 and Ozymandias. Free verse still has crafted structure; poet's GCSE note supports exploration rather than a single compulsory interpretation.
- Emigree13-1: distinguish childhood memory, received news and present hostility; unnamed country/city and speaker/poet distinction. Prereq full poem. Bright memory is not objective proof of peaceful present conditions; do not invent nationality or reduce the poem to autobiography.
- Emigree13-2: analyse recurring light, personification, changing spatial relationships and three stanzas(8/8/9lines); compare London on belonging and constraint without identifying the remembered city as London. Prereq13-1/Blake. Personified visit need not be literal travel; light does not remove final threat.
- History14-1: identify challenge to selective colonial education, named excluded figures and active identity-making; value crafted Caribbean Creole voice. Prereq full poem. Not a rejection of all historical evidence or 'incorrect English'; poetic selection is not a complete neutral textbook; avoid expanding compressed verse into false literal battle claims.

## Discover and representation/motion decision

Each unit has three concise explanation cards: connect to prior idea; model a defensible interpretation using feature→inference→qualification; then apply/compare. A reading link leads to the full assigned poem. Original summary is explicitly not evidence to quote as the poet. Three interactive views: selectable method lenses, voice/perspective contrasts and four-step movement map. This is evidence comparison/argument construction, not a physical process; keep still, user-led and keyboard accessible, no decorative3D/automatic poem film or photoreal scene suggesting a definite setting. The movement map simplifies emphasis and permits returning, not a claim every poem has a literal chronological plot.

## Investigate, practice and independent check

Before revealing lens explanations, students select a feature and test a three-option competing interpretation. Completion requires all three details, all three views and correct challenge; wrong options receive corrective feedback addressing the diagnostic misconception. Three practice items per unit with stable .practice.1–3 IDs/concepts and explanations; deliberate plausible distractors address literalisation, narrator/author confusion and deterministic technique effects.
