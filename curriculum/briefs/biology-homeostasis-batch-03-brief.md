## Purpose, statements and misconceptions
Map each statement in biology-endocrine-requirements.csv. Students identify temperature sensors/centre and hot/cold responses; Higher students explain energy transfer and shivering. Students locate six named glands, trace hormone secretion/blood transport/target action, compare nervous/hormonal timing and explain pituitary coordination. Students link insulin to glucose entry and liver/muscle glycogen storage, distinguish insulin/glucose/glycogen/glucagon, compare diabetes mechanisms/treatments, interpret supplied graphs and evaluate risk evidence without blame or causal overclaim. Higher students trace low-glucose correction and both branches of negative feedback.

Model notes distinguish tracers from real speed, generic hormone from named molecules, symbolic counts from concentrations and illustrative records from clinical data.
