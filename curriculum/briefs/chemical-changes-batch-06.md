## Scope and prerequisites
AQA Chemistry 8462 4.4.3.1–2 and Trilogy 8464 5.4.3.1–2; shared Foundation/Higher. Higher balanced half-equation extension maps 4.4.3.1/5.4.3.1. Prior ionic bonding and ion charge; oxidation/reduction for Higher.

## Learning outcomes and sequence
1. Explain why solid ionic compounds cannot conduct but molten/dissolved compounds can. Distinguish wire electrons from electrolyte ions. Identify electrode charges and ion destinations; explain discharge.
2. Predict metal and non-metal products for molten binary compounds with inert electrodes, including lead bromide. Use neutral, diatomic product formulae. Higher: balance atoms/charge and identify reduction/oxidation in supplied half-equations.
