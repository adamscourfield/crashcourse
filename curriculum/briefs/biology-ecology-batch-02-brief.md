## Learning purpose and coverage
Explain food/new-predator/new-pathogen/competition changes from context and evidence. Classify and explain structural, behavioural and functional adaptations; extreme temperature/pressure/salt and vent bacteria. Interpret producer/consumer feeding direction, photosynthetic biomass, predation and delayed cyclic population response. Estimate abundance with random-coordinate quadrats, area/density, mean/mode/median and sampling variation. Plan regular-interval transects, count consistently, measure environmental factor, repeat and explain association/confounding. Field safety/ethical handling and teacher site assessment are practical preparation, not fulfilled physical evidence. Detailed biomass, carbon/water cycles and later separate Higher environmental-distribution scope remain planned.

Prerequisites: communities/abiotic; earlier natural selection supports adaptation explanation. Misconceptions: biotic means any physical condition; individuals choose needed adaptations; functional=structure only; food arrows point towards prey; predator peak must precede prey; throw quadrat at flowers=random; small estimate=exact total; distance alone proves light caused change.

## Investigate
Three gated challenges per lesson. Biotic: scenarios, supplied counts and causal limits. Adaptations: mechanism categories, environmental context and response comparison. Food chains: producer/consumer sequence, constructed paired population graph (axis/scale/point values) and cycle interpretation. Quadrats: actual placement on a4×4 field using a pre-generated coordinate schedule, record each count, calculate scaled estimate, compare bias/precision. Four1m² samples in16m² are a small tutorial, not a universal field sample requirement; follow consistent boundary/whole-plant rules. Transects: sample fixed stations with repeated counts and light measurements, interpret mean/distance/light graph, method/hypothesis/controls/risk choices. Wrong order, wrong count, missing observations and wrong calculation/interpretation cannot complete. Drafts/selected coordinates/graph values persist across reload and route/topic switches.
