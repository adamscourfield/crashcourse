Static evidence is never animated into an unobserved history. Observe all four views and answer four diagnostic challenges: viewing alone cannot complete investigation. Wrong answers receive corrective feedback and remain retryable.

Structure icons/maps are explicitly schematics, not satellite imagery. UK outline is existing genuine Natural Earth public-domain geometry, marker approximate.
