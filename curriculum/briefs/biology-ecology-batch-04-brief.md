## Learning purpose and scope
Explain species variety versus abundance, alternative dependencies and ecosystem stability/human reliance; explain how population/living standards and improper waste handling increase pressure, and water/air/land pollutant examples. Explain habitat loss from building/quarrying/farming/dumping, peat biodiversity and carbon dioxide from decay/burning, and evaluate cheap compost/food production against conservation. Recall cattle/rice/biofuel drivers of tropical deforestation and environmental consequences. Explain CO₂/methane warming contributions, biological distribution/breeding/migration/extinction risks, consensus from systematic reviews of peer-reviewed research, and uncertainty in complex local predictions. Prerequisites: communities, material cycles; carbon before peat/deforestation. Misconceptions: abundance equals diversity; biofuel means zero impact; peat is quickly renewable; uncertainty means no evidence; one weather observation establishes climate.

## Discover and model decision
Four cards per lesson. Biodiversity uses an inspectable dependency web and original counts; evidence comparisons remain direct. Four worked looping processes: pollutant release→exposure→harm, peat extraction→oxygen exposure/decay→CO₂, forest clearing→habitat/carbon effects, greenhouse energy transfer→warming→biological response. Timings, icons, counts and pathways are symbolic, not calibrated forecasts. Inline autoplay/Loop; reduced motion paused. Accessible diagrams, text, controls, transcript; watching never completes an investigation. Selected numeric case views reveal only after prediction.

## Investigate
Three gated challenges each: contextual comparisons, ordered causal reasoning and numeric evaluation using original supplied data. Biodiversity dependency web changes when a resource disappears; alternative food routes are not guaranteed survival. Pollution traces different media and distinguishes pollutants from effects. Peat/land accounting compares habitat and a supplied fictional cost trade-off. Deforestation compares retained area and competing uses, including original percent calculations. Warming separates long-term evidence from short weather noise and uses original anomaly differences, not current climate measurements. Feedback blocks premature/wrong recording and explains each contrast. Keyboard selects/buttons/numeric fields, non-colour labels. Drafts and all previous progress persist by stable IDs.
