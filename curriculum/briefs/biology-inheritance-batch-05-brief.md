## Learning purpose and coverage
Explain four cloning methods and evaluate applications/risks; compare acquired-character and selection explanations and reasons for gradual acceptance; connect Darwin/Wallace evidence with reproductive isolation; connect Mendel's units to later chromosome/DNA research and delayed recognition; interpret fossil formation, incomplete records, relative rock order and branching relationships. Explicit full statement-to-evidence records accompany implementation. Resistance mechanism and detailed classification remain next batch.

Misconceptions: clone inherits nuclear genome from egg donor → trace labelled nuclei; identical genes guarantee identical phenotype → compare environment. Physical isolation guarantees species → require inherited divergence and inability to produce fertile offspring. Mendel knew DNA structure → order evidence historically. Missing fossils prove absence → recognise preservation/geological biases. Modern tips on a tree are not ancestors of one another.
