## Learning purpose / requirements / misconceptions
Evaluate positive/negative human impacts and competing conservation pressures: endangered-species breeding, rare-habitat protection/regeneration, margins/hedgerows within monoculture, reduced deforestation/CO₂ emissions, recycling versus landfill. Number trophic levels1–4, producers plants/algae, herbivores, secondary/tertiary carnivores and apex predators; decomposers secrete extracellular enzymes then absorb soluble products by diffusion. Construct accurately proportioned biomass pyramids from comparable data, producer at base; distinguish biomass from organism number and energy. Explain approximately1% incident light energy captured for photosynthesis versus approximately10% biomass transfer between levels, not universal constants or interchangeable units. Account for uneaten/unabsorbed material, faeces, respiration CO₂/water, urinary water/urea and glucose use; calculate percentage/fraction efficiency and link limited supply to supported numbers with individual mass/context caveats. Food security: enough food for population; birth-rate pressure, changing diets/world transport, pests/pathogens, failed rains/environment, agricultural-input cost and conflict affecting water/food; sustainable provision and population/production statistics.

Prerequisites communities/biodiversity, food chains and material cycles; trophic before pyramid, pyramid before transfer. Misconceptions: trophic levels are organism counts; decomposers ingest all intact debris; loss means atoms destroyed; mass is energy;10% exact everywhere; total food availability proves equal access. Diagnostics in stable eco5 practice/check IDs and gated investigation feedback.
