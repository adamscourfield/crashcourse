## Objectives and evidence
Construct crosses from parental genotypes by choosing gametes, entering all offspring genotypes and calculating probabilities. Family trees: interpret keys, generations and parent-child links, infer recessive carriers from unaffected parents and affected offspring, distinguish possible from certain genotypes; don't infer recessive automatically from every apparent skipped generation. Polydactyly is dominant in bounded GCSE model; CF recessive and affects cell membranes. Compare carrier versus affected and probabilities per pregnancy. Screen embryos: weigh reduced transmission risk against IVF burden, imperfect tests, costs/access, uncertainty and ethical perspectives; testing/selecting is not gene editing or a guaranteed healthy birth. Sex inheritance:23 human pairs,22 autosome pairs plus sex chromosome pair; typical XX/XY model, egg X and sperm X/Y, independent1:1 probabilities. State simplified model doesn't describe every biological variation or identity.
