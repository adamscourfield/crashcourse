## Learning purpose and misconceptions
Classify measured surroundings temperature changes, explain transfer direction and conservation, use named exothermic/endothermic examples and evaluate applications from given data. Construct/interpret reaction profiles: reactant/product energy levels, curved path, activation energy from reactants to peak, overall change between endpoints. Diagnose cold surroundings as energy leaving reaction; all reactions needing a flame; activation energy equal to overall change; horizontal axis as time; changing activation barrier changing endpoint energies.

## Discover
Three short cards each. Transfer: system/surroundings, exothermic examples/uses, endothermic examples/uses and conservation. Profiles: colliding particles/activation, relative levels/curved path, start/finish difference and trigger energy. Prerequisite ionic/chemical changes connects reaction evidence; transfer precedes profiles. Models are symbolic; temperature traces illustrative, not physical practical data; profile coordinates are relative levels with no numerical energy units, not a scale for energy calculations.

## Investigate
Transfer: three supplied initial/final temperature cases; enter signed temperature change, classify reaction and choose transfer direction before revealing result. Native fields, independent decisions, saved drafts and all3 successful cases required. Cases include neutralisation, citric acid/sodium hydrogencarbonate and combustion surroundings. Named uses reinforced in practice/check; no requirement to calculate reaction energy or enthalpy.
Profiles: three constrained drawing tasks using native controls for product level, peak height and activation-arrow origin; curve updates as controls change. Challenge targets exothermic, endothermic and changed barrier at fixed endpoints. Correct relative endpoint and peak/arrow decisions required. Live figure labels support colour; keyboard controls/no motion dependency. Constrained diagram construction is distinct from independent freehand drawing.
