## Purpose and misconceptions
Interpret controlled cell voltage/polarity data, compare reactivity, distinguish electrolyte and external electron paths, series batteries, reactant exhaustion and electrical reversal on recharge. Fuel cells require external hydrogen/oxygen, oxidise hydrogen electrochemically, produce water, and need contextual comparison with rechargeable storage. Counter voltage as universal reactivity measurement, batteries creating energy, alkaline recharging, water-only exhaust implying zero lifetime impact, and fuel cells storing all fuel internally. Higher: write balanced acidic and alkaline electrode half-equations, preserve atoms/charge, cancel electrons to the water overall equation; fuel-cell polarity differs from driven electrolysis.
