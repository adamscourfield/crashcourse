## Learning purpose and mapping
- 6.8a distribution: distinguish total water/freshwater denominators; identify major stores and uneven renewable flows. Compare USGS store bars, real five-country WDI map and groundwater transfer; independently interpret stocks/flows and map key.
- 6.8b availability-scales: compare global/national/local variation; distinguish internal renewable flows from safe access, external inflows and seasonal availability. Real five-country map plus hypothetical within-country and treatment-chain diagrams. Independently diagnose an average masking a local gap.
- 6.8c surplus-deficit: explain rainfall/evaporation, seasonal timing, recharge/geology, demand, infrastructure and environmental constraints. Four process balances: wet/dry climate, seasonal storage, abstraction/recharge, usable supply/demand. Independent fresh numerical balance and causal explanations.
- 6.8d supply-demand-change: explain population, urbanisation, irrigation, industrial change and interventions since the1970s; distinguish increased accessibility from creation of water. Dated UNESCO1980s-onward summary plus genuine UNWPP2024 future population line/range and conditional resource-per-person example. No invented half-century observational series; sampled historical evidence does not establish every year's change or a calibrated water forecast.
- 6.9a sector-use: compare USA developed, India emerging and Ethiopia developing examples with matching2022 FAO/WDI agriculture/industry/domestic shares, percentage-point calculation, composition versus amount. Independently read a neutral chart and compare fresh totals. Generalisation is conditional, not a development-stage law.
Prerequisites: resource functions/consumption and water cycle. Next:6.9b then6.10a/b and6.11a/b.
