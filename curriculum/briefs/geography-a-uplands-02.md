- Valley: changing width/depth outside the earlier dashed V-profile (corrected during screenshot review so it cannot imply adding rock into the old valley). Spur ends recede across an ice corridor; hanging stream moves over a pre-existing differential step. Static basin/lake structure after ice; not actual OS geometry.
- Roche: moving ice/debris on stoss and detached block from lee. Reverse mirrors the complete landform and particle direction, labels follow relative orientation. Exposed bedrock remains static. Corroboration needed for actual flow inference.
- Moraine: basal mixed-grain spread; terminal ridge accumulates; retreat reveals successive pause ridges only after the margin passes their position. Reset replays a teaching sequence, not backwards ice flow. Dates/distances absent.
- Streamlined forms: resistant crag and sheltered tail; sediment moulding illustration qualified as one conceptual mechanism, not a proven universal sequence. Static field shows alignment; static comparison distinguishes typical roche/drumlin form and composition.
- Land use: same conceptual rain, changed cover/pathways; established woodland infiltration vs pasture compaction, harvest/tracks/drains and settlement hard surfaces. No invented runoff totals, OS/GIS layers or claim of guaranteed flood prevention/failure.
