## Learning purpose, prerequisites, coverage and misconceptions
Macbeth units assume whole-play reading; Carol begins without requiring prior Dickens knowledge. Each student supports an interpretation with close language/structural evidence, explains development, and drafts or revises an argument. Full primary-text reading is required alongside summaries.
