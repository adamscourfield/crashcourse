## Learning purpose and coverage
Explain adrenal adrenaline in fear/stress, increased heart rate and oxygen/glucose delivery; thyroid thyroxine basal metabolism/growth/development and negative feedback. Distinguish negative from positive feedback. Explain auxin distribution and unequal growth in shoots/roots, light and gravity response. Plan light/gravity seedling comparisons; record length changes and careful labelled drawings (required practical8: AT1,3,4,7). Evaluate auxin weedkiller biodiversity effects, rooting powders and tissue culture. Explain gibberellin germination/dormancy, flowering and fruit size; ethene cell division/ripening and storage/transport control without molecular mechanisms.

Prerequisites: endocrine-system, glucagon-feedback; plant cell growth and meristems. Misconceptions: root and shoot auxin effects identical; bending is movement of whole seedling without growth; all hormones stimulate every response; weedkiller benefits have no biodiversity cost; negative feedback reinforces a rise. Prediction/reveal, ordered paths and applied checks diagnose these.
