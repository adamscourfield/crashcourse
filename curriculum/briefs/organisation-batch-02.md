## Learning purpose / Discover

Students can match enzyme/substrate/products and production sites, distinguish production from secretion/action, explain condition effects without confusing cold slowing with denaturation, calculate a rate with correct interpretation, and separate bile’s physical/pH roles from lipase catalysis.

Digestive enzymes has five cards, all dig2:enzymes: family/action map; amylase sites(stated salivary glands/pancreas/small intestine); protease sites(stomach/pancreas/small intestine); lipase sites(pancreas/small intestine); product uses. Word equations: carbohydrates→simple sugars; amylase starch→sugars; proteins→amino acids; lipids→glycerol+fatty acids. Starch summary is not a claim every fragment becomes glucose in one reaction. Pancreatic secretion acts in the intestine; food does not pass through the pancreas. Products build new carbohydrates/lipids/proteins; some glucose is used in respiration.

Conditions has five cards: dig2:temperature warming below optimum; dig2:temperature high-heat damage/cold slowing; dig2:ph enzyme-specific optima; dig2:temperature amount/time; dig2:ph comparable endpoint1/time. Rates are illustrative original data, not measured results. Warming increases motion/encounters below optimum; denaturation changes active-site suitability. Cold alone usually slows without permanent shape damage. Different enzymes have different optima, and reduced rate alone cannot prove all molecules permanently destroyed. No exact universal37°C or pH7 optimum is taught.

Bile has four cards, all dig2:bile: liver production/gallbladder storage; alkaline neutralisation of stomach HCl; physical emulsification preserving fat amount; link both effects to lipase with controlled comparison. Bile is not an enzyme and does not itself cleave lipid molecules into glycerol/fatty acids. Small droplets expose more surface; lipase supplies catalysis. Intestinal alkaline benefit is not a claim all enzymes prefer alkali.

## Misconceptions and limitations

Production site equals action site; lipase makes amino acids; products are consumed enzyme fragments; respiration equals digestion; every enzyme has one universal optimum; cold kills protein molecules; every pH reduction proves irreversible destruction;1/time equals any arbitrary chemical rate; gallbladder makes bile; emulsification chemically digests fat; more droplets create more fat; bile is lipase. Teaching and distractors diagnose these explicitly.

Enzymes are protein molecules, not living cells. Model shapes omit molecular flexibility, diffusion and complex mixtures. Bile diagrams simplify real chemistry/anatomy; observed model outcomes cannot specify patient treatment or practical measurements. Scope-specific prerequisite knowledge is supplied without claiming complete metabolism/respiration teaching.
