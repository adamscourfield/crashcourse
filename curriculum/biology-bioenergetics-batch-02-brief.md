## Objectives and sequence

1. Aerobic: continuous exothermic cellular respiration, oxygen/reactants/products and recognised formulas; energy for synthesis, movement, keeping warm. Prerequisite glucose uses.
2. Anaerobic: compare oxygen need, incomplete oxidation, much less energy, muscle lactic acid versus plant/yeast ethanol+CO₂; fermentation economics in bread/alcohol. Prerequisite aerobic.
3. Exercise: energy demand; heart rate/breathing rate/breath volume rise to deliver oxygenated blood; insufficient supply, anaerobic contribution, lactic acid/debt, fatigue; inspect original data and calculate rates. Prerequisites aerobic and anaerobic.
4. Higher recovery: order muscle→blood→liver→glucose; extra oxygen after exercise and oxygen debt. Original step-constant oxygen data supports an explicit arithmetic comparison, not a physiological forecast.
5. Metabolism: all cell/body reactions; respiration energy for continual enzyme-controlled synthesis; sugar/amino acid/fatty acid/glycerol synthesis and breakdown; glucose→starch/glycogen/cellulose;1glycerol+3fattyacids→lipid; glucose+nitrate→aminoacids→protein; excessprotein nitrogen→urea for excretion.
