## Interaction and design rules

Use close-reading details, character knowledge contrasts, scene/causal sequences, competing readings, evidence selection and argument revision. Static language evidence should remain still. An optional staged sequence may move only when it clarifies a dramatic process; if used, default inline loop/autoplay with reduced-motion pause and full controls. Rotating objects or decorative photographs are not a default Literature model.

Every batch needs a LESSON_TEMPLATE brief, official references, verified wording/scene attribution, misconceptions, targeted feedback, fresh independent questions, draft/resume behaviour and mobile/keyboard checks. Original explanations; use lawful editions and short quotations. Before reproducing protected anthology or Priestley text, establish an appropriate rights basis; an exam board’s permissions do not automatically cover this app. Public-domain Shakespeare quotations are distinguished from modern editorial commentary.
