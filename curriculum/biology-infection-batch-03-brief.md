## Learning purpose
Explain single-clone antibody specificity and hybridoma production; apply supplied diagnostic/measurement/fluorescent/targeted-delivery contexts and evaluate limits/ethics; distinguish detection from identifying cause; explain nitrate/magnesium deficiency and pests versus pathogens; classify physical, chemical and mechanical defences with all specified examples.

Prerequisites: shared human defences for monoclonals, production for uses, uses for Higher identification kits; Organisation plant tissues for both-tier pests/deficiency. Plant defences follows the both-tier plant lesson. Existing shared TMV/rose black spot lessons supply overlapping teaching; link and reuse rather than reintroducing them as different IDs.

Misconceptions: monoclonal means one antibody molecule; tumour cell alone makes the correct antibody; every yellow leaf is infection; detection alone proves a pathogen; aphids are fungi; mineral ions are energy; targeted cancer delivery guarantees no side effects; plant defences are all antibodies. Each diagnosed in activities/checks and corrected explicitly.

## Discover
Four/five concise explanation cards with3D/labelled2D. Hybridoma fusion→clone→collect/purify; antibody/antigen fit with matching/non-matching sites. Supplied assay mechanism links molecular binding to a visible band. Plant damage schematics include all seven specified signs; nitrate-limited growth and magnesium chlorophyll comparisons preserve separation from infection. Plant surface cutaways and touch/curl states link each defence to its mechanism.

Representations use symbolic shapes/scales/colours, original assay outcomes and discrete stages, not molecular structures, clinical results, botanical identification, time courses or guarantees of protection. AQA’s early side-effect/uptake evaluation is framed historically, not a claim of current usage rates. Targeting aims to minimise non-target damage but does not establish absence of adverse effects.

## Investigate
Three challenges per lesson, except four for plant defences if needed for full examples. Production: construct the valid production sequence and compare cell capabilities/specificity. Uses: step a supplied lateral-flow analogy with target absent/present/control failure; compare measurement, fluorescence and payload contexts; assess given benefit/side-effect/ethics evidence. Detection: inspect all seven signs without jumping to diagnosis; choose reference/laboratory/specific-kit evidence and interpret uncertain negative/positive cases. Nutrients/pests: compare matched nitrate and magnesium conditions, restore each independently, identify TMV/fungus/aphid/bacterial categories without testing named bacterial plant examples. Defences: remove/restore wall/cuticle/bark; compare antibacterial/poison effects; step thorn/hair/touch and mimicry examples with explicit limits.

Prediction or construction precedes reveal. Successful completion needs each comparison and correct mechanism; wrong feedback explains the distinction, saved evidence gates progression. Native controls and buttons, still by default,3D rotation/zoom and paired SVG/text, non-colour labels. Save unfinished sequence, test state and selections. No real specimen/practical competence or personal diagnostic/treatment advice.
